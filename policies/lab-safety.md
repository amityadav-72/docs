# POL-OPS-01: College Computer Lab Usage & Safety Rules

## 1. Purpose
To ensure responsible use of college computer lab facilities, protect institution hardware, and prevent network disruptions.

## 2. Scope
All student members and participants attending workshops in Central Computing Center labs at PRPCEM.

## 3. Responsible Owner
**Event Operations Lead, MSC PRPCEM**

## 4. Effective Date and Review Interval
- **Effective Date:** August 01, 2026
- **Review Interval:** 365 Calendar Days (Annual)

## 5. Mandatory Rules and Requirements
- No food or beverages permitted inside computer labs.
- Do not unplug or reconfigure institutional desktop cabling, power cords, or LAN switches.
- All temporary software installed for workshops must be uninstalled following session completion.
- Students must report any faulty equipment to the lab technician immediately.
