# Marketing & Brand Assets

The brand identity of **.NET Conf Amravati** blends the enterprise professionalism of the global Microsoft .NET ecosystem with the energetic community spirit of Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM.

This section defines the official brand guidelines, provides design tokens and typography rules, catalogs media assets, and provides ready-to-use promotional copy templates.

---

## 1. Marketing Philosophy & Strategy

Our marketing operates across three structured phases:

```
[ Phase 1: Awareness & Teasers ] ────► T-60 Days (Save-the-Date, Theme Announcement)
                 │
[ Phase 2: Engagement & CFS ] ────────► T-45 Days (Call for Speakers, Sponsor Features)
                 │
[ Phase 3: Registration Blitz ] ──────► T-25 Days (Speaker Cards, College Roadshows)
```

### Core Principles
- **Clarity Over Hype:** Focus marketing communications on verified session abstracts, speaker credentials, and practical skills.
- **Strict Brand Integrity:** Comply with Microsoft Trademark and Brand Usage guidelines.
- **Multichannel Omnipresence:** Coordinate digital campaigns (LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp communities) with physical on-campus posters across regional engineering colleges.
