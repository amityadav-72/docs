# Brand Guidelines & Visual Identity

This guide codifies the visual identity, design tokens, typography rules, and logo usage guidelines for **.NET Conf Amravati**. All organizing committees, external design partners, and media teams must adhere to these standards.

---

## 1. Color Palette & Design Tokens

The conference visual identity draws from the official **Microsoft Fluent Design System** and **.NET Brand Palette**:

### Primary Brand Palette
- **.NET Purple (`#512BD4`):** The signature global .NET identity color. Used for primary badges, keynote title cards, and hero banners.
- **Microsoft Fluent Blue (`#0078D4`):** Represents cloud innovation, Azure technology, and enterprise reliability.
- **Fluent Dark Blue (`#005A9E`):** Used for contrast typography, footers, and dark accents.
- **Canvas White (`#FFFFFF`) / Dark Void (`#0F172A`):** Clean background canvases for light and dark documentation themes.

### Microsoft 4-Color Accent System
Used for decorative accents, category tagging, and callouts:
- **Microsoft Red (`#F25022`):** Critical alerts, live stream indicators, registration deadlines.
- **Microsoft Green (`#7FBA00`):** Success badges, confirmed milestones, open registrations.
- **Microsoft Blue (`#00A4EF`):** Informational callouts, technical session tags.
- **Microsoft Yellow (`#FFB900`):** Warnings, pending actions, keynote spotlights.

## 3. Logo Assets & Safe Space Rules

### Logo Assets
- **Primary Logo:** Full lockup consisting of the stylized .NET Conf hexagonal mark paired with the text: `.NET Conf Amravati` and subtext: `Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM`.
- **Secondary Icon:** The compact square .NET emblem with the edition year (e.g., `DNCA 26`).

### Clear Space & Scale
- Maintain a minimum clear space surrounding the logo equal to the height of the `.NET` letterform (`X-height`).
- The logo must never be rendered smaller than **120 pixels wide** in digital media or **30 mm wide** in print materials.

## 4. Microsoft & .NET Trademark Dos and Don'ts

::: danger Trademark Compliance Checklist
Always respect the intellectual property of Microsoft Corporation and the .NET Foundation:
:::

### Approved Practices (DO):
- Display the official non-affiliation community disclaimer on all conference publications.
- Refer to the conference as: **".NET Conf Amravati [YEAR], an independent community event organized by Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM"**.
- Use the official .NET and Azure logos only to indicate technical topics covered in sessions.

### Prohibited Practices (DO NOT):
- Do NOT alter, tilt, distort, recolor, or add drop-shadow effects to the Microsoft 4-square logo or .NET logo.
- Do NOT use phrasing such as *"Microsoft's Official Amravati Conference"* or *"Certified by Microsoft"*.
- Do NOT combine the Microsoft corporate logo into the student club logo or create hybrid marks.
