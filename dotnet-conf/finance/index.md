### Core Accounting Policies
1. **The In-Kind Barrier Rule:** In-kind contributions (such as 500 printed delegate bags, complimentary lunch boxes, or software licenses) must be recorded with an estimated fair-market cash value for reporting, but **must never be booked as cash received**. They may not be used to offset cash obligations such as speaker travel or equipment rentals.
2. **Segregation of Financial Duties:** The student proposing an expenditure (e.g., Logistics Lead) cannot be the student who authorizes payment (Treasurer) or releases funds (Faculty Advisor).
3. **Receipt Requirement:** Zero reimbursements are disbursed without an authentic GST tax invoice or physical merchant receipt.
