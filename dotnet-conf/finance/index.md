# Finance & Budget History

::: warning Restricted Access: Financial Governance
Itemized bank vouchers, confidential corporate invoices, and internal reimbursement receipts are protected documents restricted to the College Principal, Faculty Advisor, and Student Executive Treasurer. The summaries published herein represent audited public statements.
:::

This central finance portal maintains the complete multi-year financial history of **.NET Conf Amravati**. It codifies institutional budgeting rules, reconciles cash and in-kind contributions, and audits every rupee spent to guarantee absolute transparency.

### Core Accounting Policies
1. **The In-Kind Barrier Rule:** In-kind developer grants (such as .xyz domains, JetBrains IDE vouchers, or Packt literature) must be recorded with an estimated fair-market cash value for reporting, but **must never be booked as cash received**. They cannot be converted to cash or used to offset direct expenses.
2. **Segregation of Financial Duties:** The student proposing an expenditure (e.g., Logistics Lead) cannot be the student who authorizes payment (Treasurer) or releases funds (Faculty Advisor).
3. **Receipt Requirement:** Zero reimbursements are disbursed without an authentic GST tax invoice or physical merchant receipt.
