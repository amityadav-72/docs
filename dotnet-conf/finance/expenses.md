## 4. Reimbursement Protocol
1. **Pre-Authorization:** No individual student may incur out-of-pocket expenses exceeding ₹1,000 without prior written WhatsApp/email confirmation from the Treasurer.
2. **Submission Deadline:** All physical receipts and claim forms must be submitted within **5 business days** of conference conclusion.
3. **Audit Trail:** Cash reimbursements are disbursed via institutional bank transfer directly into the student's registered bank account after faculty verification.
