## 3. Financial Risk & Reserve Fund Policy

To protect future committees against unforeseen inflationary spikes or last-minute sponsor payment delays, MSC PRPCEM maintains the **Conference Reserve Fund**:

1. **Surplus Rollover:** Any operating surplus generated during an edition is automatically locked into the Reserve Fund and may not be spent on casual club activities.
2. **Emergency Access Criteria:** The reserve fund may only be drawn upon during an active conference cycle if an approved corporate sponsor defaults on payment within 72 hours of event day.
3. **Restoration Rule:** If reserve funds are utilized, the incoming committee must structure its subsequent budget to replenish the reserve balance.
