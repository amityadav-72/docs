# Central Resource Archive

This central repository indexes all official documentation artifacts, historical proposals, bilateral corporate agreements, slide decks, and audited accounting ledgers produced across all editions of **.NET Conf Amravati**.

---

## 1. Document Index & Metadata Classification

Every archived artifact is cataloged with its unique Document ID, operational category, edition tag, creation date, designated document owner, and access classification level:

- <span class="badge badge-success">Public</span>: Unrestricted public access for attendees, students, and community members.
- <span class="badge badge-restricted">Restricted: Leadership Only</span>: Confidential financial figures, personal contact lists, or internal merchant contracts restricted to Core Leadership and Faculty Advisors.

## 3. Physical Archive Vault Location

Physical paper documentation—including counterfoils of signed corporate MoUs, certified vendor tax invoices, rubber-stamped college purchase orders, and physical attendee feedback forms—is stored in the:

- **Institutional Location:** Room 204, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, PRPCEM Campus.
- **Custodians:** Head of Department (CSE) & Faculty Advisor, Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM.
- **Retention Period:** Permanent institutional archive (minimum 7 years in accordance with university accreditation standards).

---

## 4. Digital Archive Protocol

1. **Digital Preservation:** All physical documents must be scanned at 300 DPI (PDF/A archival standard) within 7 days of signature.
2. **Naming Convention:** Files must follow the standardized naming convention: `DNCA-[YEAR]-[CATEGORY]-[DOCNAME].pdf` (e.g. `DNCA-2025-FIN-AuditedBalanceSheet.pdf`).
3. **Restricted File Handling:** Redacted public copies are uploaded to this documentation repository, while un-redacted copies containing confidential personal data and bank vouchers are secured in the restricted faculty drive.
