# Central Sponsorship & Partnerships Management

This section houses the permanent institutional system for managing corporate sponsorships, technology partnerships, community alliances, and vendor relationships across all editions of **.NET Conf Amravati**.

---

## 1. Centralized Relationship Philosophy

Unlike fragmented student events that discard contact lists at the end of each academic year, .NET Conf Amravati maintains **one single permanent organizational record** for every sponsor, partner, and vendor.

### Core Architecture Rules:
1. **One Organization, Multiple Editions:** A partner who supports multiple editions maintains a single profile in the [Sponsor Directory](/dotnet-conf/sponsors/directory) with discrete records linked to individual editions in [Sponsorship History](/dotnet-conf/sponsors/history).
2. **Clear Distinction Between Cash vs. In-Kind:** In-kind contributions (e.g. software licenses, domain grants, educational books) are tracked with their estimated fair market value but never booked as cash income. All venue and campus logistical expenses are supported institutionally by PRPCEM.
3. **Student Value First:** Partnerships focus on delivering tangible developer benefits directly to attendees without charging high registration fees.
4. **Data Protection & Role-Based Access:** Attendee contact details are strictly protected and never commercialized.

## 2. Sponsorship Categories & Taxonomy

To ensure structured engagement, partners are classified into standardized categories:

| Category | Description | Primary Contribution Type | Key Benefits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Title Sponsor** | Primary headline partner whose name is linked directly to the edition title. | High-tier Financial Contribution | Prime stage naming, keynote address, premium booth, maximum logo branding. |
| **Platinum Sponsor** | Top-tier financial supporter. | Substantial Financial Contribution | Dedicated session slot, foyer booth, full-page collateral feature. |
| **Gold Sponsor** | Mid-tier financial supporter. | Standard Financial Contribution | Banner placement, logo on badges and website, delegate kit inserts. |
| **Silver Sponsor** | Entry-level financial supporter. | Minor Financial Contribution | Logo on website, digital mentions, shared banner display. |
| **Technology Partner** | Provides developer tools, licenses, cloud credits, or APIs for workshops. | Software Licenses / Cloud Credits | Technical demonstration session, product trial distribution to students. |
| **Merchandise Partner** | Produces delegate kits, attendee t-shirts, lanyards, or badge clips. | In-Kind Manufactured Goods | Exclusive branding on attendee apparel, delegate bags, and swag. |
| **Venue Partner** | Institutional host providing auditoriums, seminar halls, and power infrastructure. | Infrastructure & Facilities | Institutional host recognition, ceremonial welcome address. |
| **Food & Hospitality** | Sponsors refreshments, delegate lunches, or VIP dining. | In-Kind or Financial Catering Subsidies | Dining pavilion branding, table placemats, snack box promotional stickers. |
| **Community Partner** | Regional developer user groups and non-profit student societies. | Cross-promotion & Delegations | Community booth, shared social media broadcasts, reciprocal event support. |
| **Knowledge Partner** | Research bodies, academic publishers, or specialized training firms. | Curriculum & Expert Resources | Panel participation, academic whitepaper distribution. |
