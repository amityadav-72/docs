1. Assign a unique Organization ID (`Org-XX`).
2. Verify business legitimacy via active website and corporate registration details.
3. Record the specific MSC student relationship owner responsible for regular communication.
4. Update the [Sponsorship History](/dotnet-conf/sponsors/history) table concurrently.
