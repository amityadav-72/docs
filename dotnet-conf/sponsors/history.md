## 3. Platform & Community Partnerships

Starting with the **2025 Edition**, Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM partnered with **Sessionize** to provide industry-grade Call for Speakers (CFS) workflows:
1. **Open & Transparent CFS:** Speakers from anywhere in India can submit session proposals through an intuitive web interface.
2. **Blind Review & Scoring:** The technical committee evaluates abstracts objectively on technical depth and live coding feasibility.
3. **Automated Agenda Publishing:** Approved session schedules with speaker headshots and bios are dynamically rendered directly on the official portal [`https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/`](https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/).
