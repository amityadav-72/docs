## 2. Multi-Year Retention & Loyalty Analysis

A key indicator of .NET Conf Amravati's operational professionalism is its **sponsor retention rate**:

```
[ 2024 Inaugural Sponsors: 3 Corporate Partners ]
                       │
                       ▼ 100% Retention Rate
[ 2025 Scaled Sponsors: 3 Returning + 2 New Partners = 5 Total ]
                       │
                       ▼ 80%+ Projected Retention
[ 2026 Target: 4 Returning Partners + 3 New Enterprise Partners ]
```

### Key Factors Behind Our 100% Year-1-to-Year-2 Retention
1. **Punctual Deliverable Fulfillment:** Every promised banner, booth, and stage acknowledgment was photographed and documented with physical proof.
2. **Transparent Post-Event Reporting:** Within 14 days of conference conclusion, every sponsor received a customized 10-page Impact & Verification Dossier containing verified attendee metrics and feedback analytics.
3. **Talent Pipeline Delivery:** Sponsors seeking recruitment directly engaged top-performing students, generating measurable hiring return on investment (ROI).
