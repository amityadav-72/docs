## 3. Partnership Management SOPs

When establishing or renewing a non-financial partnership:
1. **Clarify Resource Commitments:** Ensure mutual promotional obligations and timelines are clearly written.
2. **Designate a Student Relationship Owner:** Each partner organization must have a dedicated student point of contact to avoid miscommunication.
3. **Conduct Post-Event Debriefs:** Send formal appreciation letters and invite partner community leads to join the post-conference feedback review.
