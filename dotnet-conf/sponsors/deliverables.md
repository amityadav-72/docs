## 3. In-Kind Delivery Verification Protocols

1. **Digital Voucher Verification:** Every software license or domain voucher code must be tested for validity by the technical team prior to bulk attendee distribution.
2. **Transparent Distribution:** Codes are dispatched via registered email directly to checked-in attendees to ensure that actual participants receive the benefits.
3. **Post-Event Impact Reporting:** A concise summary detailing voucher redemption rates and student project deployments is shared back with our in-kind partners as a gesture of institutional appreciation.
