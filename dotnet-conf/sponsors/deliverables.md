## 3. Protocol for Overdue Deliverables

If a deliverable status transitions to <span class="badge badge-danger">Overdue</span>:
1. **Immediate Notification:** The responsible student lead must report to the General Chair within 4 hours.
2. **Corrective Action Plan:** Deploy reserve volunteers or alternative resources (e.g. overnight digital printing) to rectify the failure before conference morning.
3. **Sponsor Transparency:** If a physical deliverable cannot be completed due to external vendor default, the sponsor must be informed immediately with an offer of equivalent compensatory value (e.g. upgraded digital broadcasting or priority stage time).
