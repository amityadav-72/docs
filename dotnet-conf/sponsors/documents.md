# Sponsorship Documents & Templates

This page provides the standardized templates, document structures, and legal wording used for corporate sponsorship acquisition, contracting, invoicing, and reporting across all editions of **.NET Conf Amravati**.

---

## 1. Sponsorship Deck Architecture

The official **.NET Conf Amravati Sponsorship Prospectus** is a 10-slide high-impact PDF deck compiled annually. Organizing teams must adhere to this proven structure:

```
Slide 1: Executive Title & Visual Branding (.NET Conf Amravati | Date & Venue)
Slide 2: About the Conference & The Vidarbha Developer Opportunity
Slide 3: Past Edition Highlights & Verified Demographics (Attendees, Colleges, Reach)
Slide 4: Why Sponsor? (Talent Pipeline, Brand Equity, CSR Tech Empowerment)
Slide 5: Keynote Speakers & Expert Profile Highlights
Slide 6: Sponsorship Tier Matrix (Title, Platinum, Gold, Silver, In-Kind)
Slide 7: Exhibition Booth Floor Plan & Foyer Visibility Map
Slide 8: Event-Day Run Sheet & Delegate Profile Breakdown
Slide 9: Institutional Credentials & College Approvals (PRPCEM Accreditation)
Slide 10: Formal Contact & Next Steps (Club Execom & Faculty Advisory)
```

## 3. Formal Sponsorship Agreement (MoU) Boilerplate

All corporate sponsorships must be ratified through a formal Memorandum of Understanding containing the following standard clauses:

1. **Parties Involved:** P. R. Pote (Patil) College of Engineering and Management (Host) and [Sponsor Legal Name] (Sponsor).
2. **Term & Scope:** Specifically designates the applicable conference edition year.
3. **Financial Commitment:** Exact payment amount, mode of transfer, and milestone dates (minimum 50% advance upon signing, 50% balance 7 days prior to event).
4. **Deliverables Enumeration:** Exhaustive bulleted list of all agreed benefits (stage time, booth dimensions, logo sizes, banners).
5. **Attendee Data Privacy Clause:** Explicit stipulation that personal attendee contact numbers and unconsented private records will not be transferred.
6. **Cancellation & Force Majeure:** Mutual refund and rescheduling policies in the event of unforeseen regional disruptions or institutional rescheduling.
7. **Signatures:** Signed by the College Principal / Faculty Advisor and the Authorized Corporate Officer.

## 4. Post-Event Sponsor Impact Dossier Guidelines

Within 14 business days following the event, the Sponsorship Team must compile and dispatch a personalized **Sponsor Impact Report**:
- Verified attendance statistics (total checked-in vs registered).
- Photographic evidence of all delivered benefits (stage backdrop, booth crowd, banners, badges).
- Summary of attendee feedback ratings relevant to sponsor sessions or booths.
- Formal Letter of Appreciation signed by the College Principal.
- Early renewal invitation for the subsequent conference edition.
