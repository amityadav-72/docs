## 3. Unsuccessful Outreach Records & Retrospective Analysis

Future committees must study these past unsuccessful engagements to understand why corporate prospects declined or stalled, avoiding unproductive repetitive outreach:

### Case 1: Multinational FinTech Enterprise (Mumbai Office) — Outreach 2024
- **Date Contacted:** January 2024
- **Channel:** LinkedIn message to Senior Director of Engineering
- **Outcome:** Declined after preliminary discussion
- **Root Cause Analysis:** The company operates on a strict global fiscal calendar running January–December, with all community and CSR sponsorship budgets locked by October of the previous year. Our outreach in January occurred 3 months too late.
- **Institutional Learning:** For enterprise and multinational corporations, outreach proposals must be dispatched during **September–October** for events planned in Q1 of the following year.

### Case 2: National E-Commerce Platform — Outreach 2025
- **Date Contacted:** November 2024
- **Channel:** Email to University Relations Desk
- **Outcome:** Incomplete / Ghosted after Stage 6 (Proposal submission)
- **Root Cause Analysis:** The sponsorship pitch was sent as a generic generic club prospectus without highlighting specific recruitment ROI, university demographics, or verified prior year attendance data.
- **Institutional Learning:** Large enterprises require quantitative hiring data: number of final-year graduates, specific technical proficiencies (.NET, C#, SQL, Azure), and exact booth floor plans.

### Case 3: Regional Web Hosting Reseller — Outreach 2024
- **Date Contacted:** February 2024
- **Outcome:** Dropped during negotiation
- **Root Cause Analysis:** The vendor insisted on obtaining exclusive rights to direct attendee email marketing and physical phone lists. MSC PRPCEM leadership strictly refused due to attendee data privacy principles and student protection rules.
- **Institutional Learning:** Never compromise attendee privacy for funding. The club's policy strictly prohibits sharing attendee phone numbers or un-anonymized databases with commercial sponsors under any circumstances.

## 4. Pipeline Execution Guidelines
1. **Log Every Interaction:** All emails, meeting notes, and verbal commitments must be logged within 24 hours.
2. **Never Over-Promise:** Only commit to deliverables listed in the approved [Sponsorship Packages](/dotnet-conf/sponsors/packages).
3. **Escalation Trigger:** If a confirmed sponsor's payment remains overdue 14 days prior to event day, immediately escalate to the Faculty Advisor.
