## 2. Specialized Non-Financial Partner Packages

In addition to cash sponsorship tiers, the conference recognizes critical operational partnerships:

### Technology Partner (Cloud / Tool Licensing)
- **Contribution Requirement:** Complimentary cloud credits, developer software licenses, or API access for all registered attendees (minimum commercial valuation equivalent of ₹25,000).
- **Entitled Benefits:** Technical workshop slot (45 minutes), product demo video looped during intervals, logo on official website and slide decks.

### Merchandise Partner (Apparel & Swag)
- **Contribution Requirement:** Production and delivery of 500+ customized delegate kits, printed conference lanyards, or volunteer t-shirts.
- **Entitled Benefits:** Prominent logo screen-printed on the physical merchandise item, acknowledgment in closing ceremony, display table in the reception lobby.

### Media & Community Partners
- **Contribution Requirement:** Minimum 3 promotional broadcasts across regional developer channels, newsletter features, or local press coverage.
- **Entitled Benefits:** Logo on conference website under *"Community Partners"*, cross-promotional mention, 2 complimentary delegate tickets.

## 3. Package Pricing Configurability Rules

1. **Annual Inflation & Budget Alignment:** Package benchmark pricing may be revised upward by up to 20% per academic year upon approval by the Faculty Advisor to account for increasing venue or catering costs.
2. **Multi-Year Commitment Discounts:** Organizations signing a binding 2-year or 3-year sponsorship MoU receive a 15% discount on their subsequent edition contributions.
3. **No Unapproved Concessions:** Student organizers may not grant additional booth square footage, speaking slots, or custom branding concessions without prior written authorization from the Event Chair and Faculty Advisor.

---

## 4. Governance & Entitlement Clause

::: danger Crucial Rule: Written Contract Requirement
A sponsor or partner is **NEVER entitled to any benefit** (verbal or implied) unless that specific deliverable is expressly enumerated in the bilaterally signed Sponsorship Agreement or Memorandum of Understanding. Verbal assurances from student volunteers are strictly non-binding.
:::
