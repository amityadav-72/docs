## 1. 2025 Edition Consolidated Executive Report

- **Event Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2025
- **Execution Date:** January 7–9, 2026 | 3-Day Technical Conference
- **Venue:** Seminar Hall, PRPCEM Amravati
- **Format:** In-Person, 3-Day Conference featuring Microsoft Employees & Microsoft MVPs
- **Theme:** *Upcoming .NET 10, Cloud-Native Azure & Generative AI (GitHub, Azure, DevOps, Power Platform)*
- **Leadership:** General Chair (President, MSC PRPCEM); Technical Director; Faculty Advisor

### Executive Summary
The 2025 edition marked the continued success of .NET Conf Amravati as an established regional conference. The event welcomed **180+ verified in-person delegates**, representing engineering and computer application institutions across Maharashtra. 

### Key Outcomes & Metrics
- **Sessions Delivered:** 3 keynote technical sessions covering upcoming .NET 10 preview architecture, Enterprise Scalability, Cloud-Native Microservices, and Generative AI with Semantic Kernel.
- **Speaker Roster:** Hosted 3 distinguished speakers (Microsoft Employees & Microsoft MVPs).
- **Financial Result:** 100% supported institutionally by PRPCEM (₹0 external corporate cash sponsorships) alongside in-kind developer grants from .xyz Domains (200 free domains) and JetBrains (4 IDE vouchers), with Sessionize community platform management.
- **Participant CSAT Rating:** 4.8 / 5.0 across digital feedback surveys.
- **Developer Impact:** 200 student delegates received free custom .xyz domains, 4 delegates won JetBrains professional IDE licenses, and attendees engaged in deep architectural Q&A.

## 3. Reporting Protocol for Future Editions

Following the conclusion of every future conference edition:
1. The Documentation Head compiles the draft report within **10 business days**.
2. The report is verified against audited financial vouchers by the Treasurer and Faculty Advisor.
3. The College Principal signs off on the executive summary.
4. The final verified report is published to this archive.
