# Consolidated Edition Reports

This repository archives the consolidated post-event executive reports for every edition of **.NET Conf Amravati**. Each report provides a holistic summary of event execution, financial performance, participant metrics, and institutional achievements.

---

## 1. 2025 Edition Consolidated Executive Report

- **Event Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2025
- **Execution Date:** Saturday, February 22, 2025 | 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM IST
- **Venue:** Central Auditorium Complex & Seminar Hall A, PRPCEM Amravati
- **Format:** In-Person, Dual-Track Conference with Sponsor Recruitment Lounge
- **Theme:** *Enterprise Scalability with .NET 9 & Azure*
- **Leadership:** General Chair (President, MSC PRPCEM); Technical Director; Faculty Advisor

### Executive Summary
The 2025 edition marked the transition of .NET Conf Amravati from a campus symposium into a regional multi-track conference. The event registered 710 applicants and welcomed **492 verified in-person delegates**, representing 14 engineering and computer application institutions across Maharashtra. 

### Key Outcomes & Metrics
- **Sessions Delivered:** 9 technical sessions across two parallel tracks (Enterprise Architecture vs AI & Cloud).
- **Speaker Roster:** Hosted 1 Microsoft MVP and 5 Principal/Senior Architects from Pune, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru.
- **Financial Result:** Generated ₹85,000 in corporate sponsorship revenue against ₹83,400 in direct operating expenses, yielding a **net operating surplus of ₹1,600**.
- **Participant CSAT Rating:** 4.8 / 5.0 across 340 submitted exit surveys.
- **Career & Recruitment Impact:** 8 student delegates secured preliminary interviews or internship offers with visiting corporate sponsors (Apex Software & Zenith Cloud).
- **Complete Reference:** [Read the Full 2025 Edition Dossier](/dotnet-conf/editions/2025)

## 2. 2024 Inaugural Edition Consolidated Executive Report

- **Event Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2024
- **Execution Date:** Saturday, March 16, 2024 | 09:00 AM – 05:30 PM IST
- **Venue:** Central Auditorium, PRPCEM Amravati
- **Format:** In-Person, Single-Track Intensive Symposium
- **Theme:** *Igniting Modern .NET 8 & Cloud Horizons*

### Executive Summary
The inaugural 2024 edition established the proof of concept for an industry-level developer conference in Amravati. Conceived and delivered entirely by student volunteers of Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM, the conference registered 520 students and achieved a verified attendance of **385 delegates**.

### Key Outcomes & Metrics
- **Sessions Delivered:** 6 intensive technical lectures covering modern .NET 8, C# 12, ASP.NET Core, EF Core 8, and Azure Container Apps.
- **Financial Result:** 100% self-funded via ₹45,000 in regional corporate sponsorships, operating at a perfectly balanced zero-deficit budget without charging delegate registration fees.
- **Participant CSAT Rating:** 4.6 / 5.0 across 210 exit evaluations.
- **Institutional Validation:** Awarded the Best Student Technical Initiative of the Year by the College Academic Council.
- **Complete Reference:** [Read the Full 2024 Edition Dossier](/dotnet-conf/editions/2024)

## 3. Reporting Protocol for Future Editions

Following the conclusion of every future conference edition:
1. The Documentation Head compiles the draft report within **10 business days**.
2. The report is verified against audited financial vouchers by the Treasurer and Faculty Advisor.
3. The College Principal signs off on the executive summary.
4. The final verified report is published to this archive.
