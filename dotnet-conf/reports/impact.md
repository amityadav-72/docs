## 3. Educational & Skill Outcomes

Exit surveys administered at the conclusion of technical sessions measured concrete skill acquisition across key paradigms:

- **92% of attendees** reported understanding the architectural differences between .NET Framework (legacy) and modern cross-platform .NET 8/9.
- **84% of attendees** reported confidence in deploying an ASP.NET Core Minimal API to containerized cloud environments (Docker / Azure).
- **78% of attendees** reported hands-on familiarity with Microsoft Semantic Kernel and AI plugin orchestration.

---

## 4. Career & Industry Recruitment Outcomes

The introduction of the dedicated **Sponsor Recruitment Pavilion** in 2025 generated measurable career outcomes:

- **Resumes Screened:** Over 240 student resumes reviewed by talent acquisition leads from visiting corporate sponsors.
- **Direct Internship Offers:** 8 final-year and pre-final-year students received immediate interview calls or paid internship offers.
- **Placement Cell Endorsement:** Commended by the College Training & Placement Officer (TPO) as the highest-converting student-led technical networking initiative on campus.

## 5. Post-Conference Community Continuity

The momentum generated by the annual conference sustains year-round community learning:
- **Weekly Study Jams:** Over 60 students joined the post-conference Saturday C# & Cloud Study Circles.
- **Open-Source Participation:** 40+ student pull requests merged into open-source repositories within 30 days of the conference.
- **Club Membership Influx:** Active MSC PRPCEM membership increased by 45% following each conference edition.
