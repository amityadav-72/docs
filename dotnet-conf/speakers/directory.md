## 2. Speaker Network Governance

1. **Annual Engagement Refresh:** At the start of every conference planning cycle (October), the Technical Committee reviews past ratings and reaches out to top-rated speakers to gauge availability.
2. **Diversity & Representation:** The committee actively seeks diverse voices across gender, geography, and industry domains, targeting at least 35%+ female representation across speaker lineups.
3. **Student Mentor Track:** Senior alumni of PRPCEM working in reputable .NET engineering roles are invited to co-present with industry veterans to inspire current students.
