# Speaker & Expert Network

A cornerstone of **.NET Conf Amravati's** regional prestige is its curated network of high-caliber speakers, including Microsoft Most Valuable Professionals (MVPs), Microsoft Certified Trainers (MCTs), Principal Cloud Architects, and open-source maintainers.

This section maintains the permanent directory of speakers across all conference editions, archives session technical materials, and provides standard templates for speaker outreach and coordination.

---

## 1. Speaker Curation & Technical Standards

The Technical Program Committee enforces strict quality criteria for all accepted sessions:

<div class="pillar-grid">
  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-num">CRITERION 01</div>
    <div class="pillar-title">Deep Technical Substance</div>
    <p class="pillar-desc">Minimum 60% of session time must be dedicated to real-world code, architecture, or performance benchmarks.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-num">CRITERION 02</div>
    <div class="pillar-title">Live Code Demonstrations</div>
    <p class="pillar-desc">Real-time terminal, IDE, or cloud console execution is mandatory; pure slide presentations are not accepted.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-num">CRITERION 03</div>
    <div class="pillar-title">Architectural Trade-Offs</div>
    <p class="pillar-desc">Sessions must explain real-world scalability, latency, operational cost, and zero-trust security trade-offs.</p>
  </div>
</div>

### Prohibited Content
- Pure sales pitches, product advertisements, or marketing slides.
- Generic introductory programming syntax covered in standard second-year university courses.
- Unverified benchmarks or proprietary claims without reproducible demonstrations.

## 3. Speaker Experience & Hospitality Commitment

MSC PRPCEM treats invited speakers with the highest institutional hospitality:
- **Dedicated Student Escort:** Every outstation speaker is assigned a dedicated senior student liaison from the moment of arrival at the railway station / airport until departure.
- **Honorarium & Travel Subsidies:** Outstation travel and premium lodging are fully covered or subsidized as per agreed conference terms.
- **Stage Technical Verification:** Minimum 30 minutes of pre-session audio, video, and screen-sharing calibration in the auditorium before stage delivery.
- **Custom Institutional Memento:** Handcrafted regional commemorative memento and formal Letter of Appreciation presented on stage by institutional leadership.
