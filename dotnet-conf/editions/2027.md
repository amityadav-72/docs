## 1. Edition Overview & Vision

- **Working Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2027
- **Edition Code:** `DNCA-2027`
- **Projected Date:** Q1 2027 (February / March 2027)
- **Target Scale:** 750+ On-site Delegates, 2-Day Hybrid Model
- **Proposed Theme:** *Decentralized Microservices, Edge AI & Next-Gen .NET Runtimes*
- **Convening Body:** Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM Incoming Leadership
- **Status:** <span class="badge badge-warning">Strategic Concept & Advance Charter</span>
- **Strategic Goal:** Transform .NET Conf Amravati from a single-day regional symposium into a multi-day State-Level Developer Festival incorporating an open 24-hour hackathon, an enterprise recruitment pavilion, and national community roundtables.

## 3. Proposed Session Tracks

1. **Track 1: Cloud-Native Microservices & Distributed High-Throughput Systems**
   - High-performance gRPC, event streaming with Kafka, distributed caches with Redis, and zero-trust identity architectures.
2. **Track 2: Edge Computing, WebAssembly & Client Experiences**
   - .NET running on WASM, embedded IoT devices, multi-platform applications on mobile and desktop.
3. **Track 3: Autonomous AI Agents & Cloud Intelligence**
   - Autonomous multi-agent swarms, local small-language models (SLMs) in production, and enterprise RAG.
4. **Special Arena: 24-Hour Student Cloud Hackathon**
   - Solving regional challenges (smart agriculture, telemedicine, education accessibility) using modern .NET and Microsoft Azure.

---

## 4. Advance Sponsorship Strategy

- **Early Commitment Program:** Establish early-bird discount packages for 2025 and 2026 returning sponsors who commit 6 months in advance.
- **CSR Technology Grants:** Explore formal Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) funding from multinational technology foundations focused on skill development in tier-2 engineering institutions.
- **Recruitment Partnerships:** Formalize an "On-Campus Talent Acquisition" pass allowing corporate engineering recruiters private interviewing suites during the conference.

---

## 5. Logistics & Multi-Day Infrastructure

- **Multi-Day Accommodation:** Partner with regional hospitality providers to offer subsidized student dormitory and hotel lodging packages for delegates traveling from Pune, Nagpur, Aurangabad, and Mumbai.
- **Stage Upgrades:** Introduce professional multi-camera live video broadcasting across all sessions to reach an estimated 5,000+ virtual attendees across India.
