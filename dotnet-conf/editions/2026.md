## 1. Edition Overview

- **Official Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2026
- **Edition Tag:** `DNCA-2026`
- **Scheduled Date:** Friday, December 25, 2026 (Christmas Public Holiday)
- **Target Duration:** 1-Day Mega Conference | 09:00 AM – 06:30 PM IST
- **Venue:** PRPCEM Campus, Amravati
- **Event Format:** In-Person 1-Day Mega Conference featuring Multi-Track Technical Sessions, Hands-on Demos, and Technology Showcase
- **Working Theme:** *Modern Microsoft Ecosystem: Upcoming .NET, GitHub, Azure, AI, DevOps & Power Platform*
- **Convening Body:** Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM (MSC PRPCEM)
- **Current Preparation Status:** <span class="badge badge-info">Phase 2: Outreach & Partnerships Active</span>
- **Official Portal:** [https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/](https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/)
- **Strategic Format Rationale:** Based on operational retrospectives from the 2024 and 2025 3-day weekday symposia—where invited industry speakers (Microsoft Employees and MVPs) faced corporate leave constraints on Thursdays/Fridays and students had academic classes—the 2026 edition transitioned to an intensive **1-Day Mega Conference on December 25, 2026 (Public Holiday)**. This guarantees frictionless participation for outstation corporate speakers and student delegates without weekday work or lecture conflicts.
- **Core Objectives:**
  1. Preview the next frontier of Microsoft .NET alongside the comprehensive ecosystem: GitHub Actions CI/CD, Microsoft Azure Cloud-Native, Artificial Intelligence & Multi-Agent workflows, DevOps, and Power Platform.
  2. Eliminate corporate weekday leave barriers for invited speakers by hosting on a national holiday.
  3. Host 500 target student developers and aspiring software engineers from across Maharashtra.
