## 4. Standard Edition Documentation Structure

Every edition follows an identical 11-section documentation standard to maintain consistency and ease of historical comparison:

1. **Edition Overview:** Dates, venue, theme, governance, and target goals.
2. **Planning & Preparation:** Meeting minutes, timelines, approvals, and team assignments.
3. **Speakers & Sessions:** Speaker profiles, abstracts, slide decks, and feedback.
4. **Sponsorship & Partnerships:** Confirmed partners, package fulfillment, and agreements.
5. **Marketing & Outreach:** Campaigns, posters, media reach, and conversion stats.
6. **Logistics & Operations:** Infrastructure, catering, AV equipment, and venue setup.
7. **Finance & Reconciliation:** Estimated vs actual budget, revenue, and receipts.
8. **Event-Day Execution:** Run sheet, live session logs, coordination, and photo gallery.
9. **Results & Impact:** Verified attendance, demographics, feedback scores, and reach.
10. **Retrospective:** What worked, what failed, operational bottlenecks, and learnings.
11. **Final Consolidated Report:** Executive summary and institutional sign-off.
