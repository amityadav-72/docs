## 1. Edition Overview

- **Official Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2025
- **Edition Tag:** `DNCA-2025`
- **Date & Duration:** Saturday, February 22, 2025 | 09:00 AM – 06:00 PM IST (Full Day)
- **Venue:** Central Auditorium Complex & Seminar Hall A, PRPCEM Amravati
- **Format:** Dual-Track In-Person Conference (Track 1: Enterprise Web & Microservices; Track 2: Cloud, DevOps & AI)
- **Theme:** *Enterprise Scalability with .NET 9 & Azure*
- **Convening Body:** Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM in association with Department of CSE & IT
- **Official Website:** `mscprpcem.tech/events/dotnetconf-2025`
- **Core Objectives:**
  1. Highlight .NET 9 performance enhancements, Native AOT in ASP.NET Core, and server-side Blazor optimizations.
  2. Implement a dual-track architecture to serve both early-stage engineering students and advanced industry practitioners.
  3. Expand regional reach across 14 engineering campuses across Vidarbha (SGBAU & RTMNU).

## 3. Speakers & Sessions (Dual-Track)

### Track A: Enterprise Architecture & Modern .NET (Main Auditorium)
1. **Keynote: What's Next in .NET 9 & Cloud-Native Runtimes:** Architectural benchmarks, JIT enhancements, and C# 13 features. *(Delivered by Principal Enterprise Architect)*
2. **High-Throughput Microservices with ASP.NET Core & gRPC:** Building distributed event-driven systems using RabbitMQ and MassTransit. *(Delivered by Senior Cloud Consultant)*
3. **Advanced Blazor & Hybrid Architecture in .NET 9:** Full-stack C# web development with streaming server rendering and web components. *(Delivered by Web Practice Lead)*
4. **Resilience Engineering with Polly & OpenTelemetry:** Distributed tracing, metric collection, and fault injection in production. *(Delivered by SRE Director)*

### Track B: Cloud, DevOps & AI Integration (Seminar Hall A)
1. **Generative AI with C# & Semantic Kernel:** Integrating Azure OpenAI and local LLMs directly into .NET applications. *(Delivered by AI/ML Solutions Architect)*
2. **Containerized .NET on Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS):** Automated deployment with GitHub Actions, Helm charts, and ingress routing. *(Delivered by Cloud Architect)*
3. **Secure Identity in Modern Web Apps:** OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, and Entra ID implementation patterns. *(Delivered by Security Practice Head)*
4. **Interactive Workshop: Building an End-to-End Microservice in 90 Minutes:** Hands-on guided lab for 80 pre-registered student developers. *(Delivered by MSC PRPCEM Tech Leads)*

## 10. Retrospective

### Successes
- Dual-track structure was universally praised; allowed advanced attendees and beginners to choose their pace.
- QR-code check-in reduced morning queue wait times to under 12 seconds per person.
- The sponsor lounge provided genuine recruitment utility for participating companies.

### Areas for Improvement
- Lunch crowd flow in the central courtyard created a 15-minute congestion bottleneck.
- Track B venue filled to capacity during the Semantic Kernel session, requiring overflow seating.

### Recommendations for 2026 Edition
- Provision a third track or larger secondary auditorium for AI-centric workshops.
- Implement staggered lunch timing across tracks to eliminate dining bottlenecks.
