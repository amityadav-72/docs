## 10. Retrospective

### Successes
- High engagement during the 3-day format; allowed delegates to interact extensively with Microsoft Employees and MVPs.
- QR-code check-in reduced morning queue wait times to under 12 seconds per person.
- The sponsor lounge provided genuine recruitment utility for participating companies.

### Areas for Improvement
- **The 3-Day Weekday Speaker Travel Bottleneck:** With the conference running Wednesday to Friday (Jan 7–9, 2026), invited industry experts (Microsoft Employees and MVPs) faced corporate leave constraints and client deadlines on working weekdays (Thursday and Friday). Traveling to Amravati mid-week caused acute logistics friction for outstation speakers, while student delegates encountered timetable clashes with regular college lab sessions.
- Lunch crowd flow in the central courtyard created a temporary congestion bottleneck.
- Seminar Hall filled to capacity during the Semantic Kernel session, requiring overflow seating.

### Recommendations for 2026 Edition
- Transition to an intensive 1-Day Mega Conference on December 25, 2026 (Christmas Public Holiday) to permanently eliminate weekday corporate leave conflicts for outstation speakers and class timetable conflicts for 500 student delegates.
- Implement staggered lunch timing across tracks to eliminate dining bottlenecks.
