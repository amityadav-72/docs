## 1. Edition Overview

- **Official Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2024
- **Edition Tag:** `DNCA-2024`
- **Date & Duration:** January 9–11, 2025 | 3-Day Technical Symposium
- **Venue:** Seminar Hall, P. R. Pote (Patil) College of Engineering and Management, Amravati
- **Format:** In-Person, 3-Day Intensive Technical Symposium
- **Theme:** *Upcoming .NET 9 & The Modern Microsoft Ecosystem (GitHub, Azure, AI, DevOps, Power Platform)*
- **Convening Body:** Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM (MSC PRPCEM)
- **Official Portal:** [https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/](https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/)
- **Core Objectives:**
  1. Preview the upcoming innovations in .NET 9 alongside foundational .NET 8 LTS enterprise patterns.
  2. Expand beyond C# into the broader modern Microsoft ecosystem: GitHub Actions CI/CD, Microsoft Azure Cloud-Native, AI technologies, DevOps automation, and Power Platform.
  3. Deliver practical live coding and architectural guidance by 5 expert speakers (Microsoft Employees & Microsoft MVPs).

## 10. Retrospective

### What Worked Exceptionally Well
- High speaker quality; attendees praised hands-on live coding by Microsoft Employees and MVPs.
- Strict adherence to the published timetable across all 3 days.
- Zero registration fee barrier enabled students from all economic backgrounds to participate.

### Challenges & Bottlenecks
- **3-Day Weekday Scheduling & Speaker Travel Friction:** Because the 3-day symposium ran Thursday to Saturday (Jan 9–11, 2025), outstation speakers (Microsoft Employees and MVPs) faced significant corporate work commitments on Thursday and Friday. Coordinating their mid-week corporate leaves and long-distance travel to Amravati was challenging. Additionally, weekday college lectures created attendance conflicts for visiting student delegates.
- Seminar Hall seating filled quickly during afternoon sessions.
- Wi-Fi connectivity in the back rows experienced intermittent packet loss during peak live demos.

### Lessons Learned for Next Edition (2025)
- Deploy dedicated mesh Wi-Fi 6 access points across all seating tiers in the Seminar Hall.
- Implement automated QR check-in to speed up morning entries.
- Closely evaluate scheduling formats to reduce weekday corporate travel friction for outstation speakers.
