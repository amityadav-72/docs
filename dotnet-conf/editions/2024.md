## 1. Edition Overview

- **Official Title:** .NET Conf Amravati 2024
- **Edition Tag:** `DNCA-2024`
- **Date & Duration:** Saturday, March 16, 2024 | 09:00 AM – 05:30 PM IST (Full Day)
- **Venue:** Main Central Auditorium, P. R. Pote (Patil) College of Engineering and Management, Amravati
- **Format:** In-Person, Single-Track Intensive Symposium
- **Theme:** *Igniting Modern .NET 8 & Cloud Horizons*
- **Convening Body:** Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM in association with Department of CSE & IT
- **Official Portal:** `mscprpcem.tech/events/dotnetconf-2024`
- **Core Objectives:**
  1. Introduce the regional student community to the long-term support release of Microsoft .NET 8.
  2. Demonstrate modern C# 12 language enhancements, ASP.NET Core Web APIs, and Entity Framework Core 8.
  3. Prove the viability of hosting an industry-standard, fully sponsored developer conference in Amravati.

## 10. Retrospective

### What Worked Exceptionally Well
- High speaker quality; attendees praised the hands-on live coding over passive slide decks.
- Strict adherence to the published timetable; all sessions started and finished within a 5-minute tolerance.
- Zero registration fee barrier enabled students from all economic backgrounds to participate.

### Challenges & Bottlenecks
- Single-track constraint meant some advanced attendees found introductory sections basic, while beginners struggled during deep microservices sessions.
- Wi-Fi connectivity in the balcony seating area experienced intermittent packet loss during the afternoon peak.

### Lessons Learned for Next Edition (2025)
- Transition to a **Dual-Track structure** (Beginner / Core Track vs Advanced / Architecture Track).
- Deploy multiple high-density Wi-Fi access points across all seating tiers.
- Introduce an official sponsor booth exhibition area in the foyer.
