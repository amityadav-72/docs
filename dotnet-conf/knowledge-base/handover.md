# Edition Handover Guide & Continuity Manual

This manual governs the formal handover protocol between the outgoing organizing leadership and the incoming student executive committee. It guarantees that **.NET Conf Amravati** retains its institutional memory and continues to scale year after year.

---

## 1. What the Next Organizing Team Should Know

To the incoming Executive Committee: **Congratulations on taking the reins of Vidarbha's largest developer conference.** As you begin planning your edition, keep these hard-won operational realities at the front of your mind:

### 1.1 The Golden Rules of .NET Conf Amravati
1. **Start 5 Months in Advance:** You cannot pull off a 600-person conference in 4 weeks. If you wait until January to start contacting sponsors and speakers, you will face rejected proposals and unmanageable stress. Begin your charter in **September or October**.
2. **Sponsors Are Partners, Not Piggy Banks:** Corporate sponsors do not give funds out of charity; they invest in student talent recruitment and brand prestige. Treat their recruiters like VIPs, deliver on every promised benefit, and give them a dedicated recruitment lounge.
3. **The Speaker Experience is Your Reputation:** Renowned Microsoft MVPs and Principal Cloud Architects travel to Amravati on their personal time. If you treat them with warmth, ensure their stage tech runs flawlessly, and assign them an attentive student escort, they will return every year and recommend us to their peers.
4. **Never Compromise Attendee Privacy:** Commercial agencies and recruiters will frequently ask for your attendee phone numbers. Refuse every time. MSC PRPCEM's reputation for ethical stewardship is non-negotiable.
5. **Document Everything as You Go:** Do not wait until after exams or graduation to write meeting minutes, collect invoices, or update this portal. Update records weekly.

## 3. Institutional Host & Partner Relationship Notes

When coordinating with institutional leadership and in-kind partners for future editions, review their collaboration preferences:

- **PRPCEM College Administration:** Coordinate with Principal and Faculty Advisor in early September. Submit venue requisition for Seminar Hall / campus auditorium facilities, AV system checks, electrical backup, and college guest house accommodation for visiting Microsoft Employees and MVPs.
- **.xyz Domains:** Contact developer relations team for student custom domain grants (200 in 2025, 500+ in 2026). Ensure attendee redemption codes and custom domain claiming guidelines are distributed smoothly during onboarding.
- **JetBrains:** Reach out through academic and community sponsorship programs for IDE vouchers (4 vouchers in 2025, 6 vouchers in 2026). Coordinate distribution for quiz toppers and hackathon participants.
- **Packt Publishing:** Coordinate technical book voucher grants and digital developer guides for workshop attendees and top contributors.
- **Sessionize:** Official community partner for Call for Speakers (CFS) and schedule management (active from 2025 edition onwards). Set up event dashboard, speaker profile links, and interactive live timetable on [`https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/`](https://dnc.mscprpcem.tech/).
