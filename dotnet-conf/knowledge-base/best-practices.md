# Conference Best Practices Playbooks

Over multiple successful editions of **.NET Conf Amravati**, Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM has developed repeatable, high-performing playbooks that consistently deliver exceptional results.

---

## 1. The Sponsor Delight Playbook

How to maintain a 100% sponsor retention rate:
1. **The 24-Hour Confirmation Email:** Immediately upon verbal or written sponsorship commitment, send a formal acknowledgment with bank remittance details and a clean vector asset request form.
2. **Dedicated Sponsor Concierge:** Assign a specific, well-trained student volunteer to each sponsor company. The concierge greets them upon arrival, escorts them to their designated booth, assists with banner setup, and brings them hot tea and water throughout the day.
3. **The 14-Day Post-Event Dossier:** Deliver a comprehensive [Template 19: Sponsor Impact Report](/dotnet-conf/knowledge-base/templates) containing high-resolution photos of their booth, stage logos, and candidate interaction statistics within two weeks.

---

## 2. The Speaker Hospitality Playbook

How to ensure world-class Microsoft MVPs and architects love speaking in Amravati:
1. **Station/Airport Reception:** Never let an outstation speaker navigate regional transit alone. A student liaison in official club attire should be waiting at the platform/gate with a welcome placard.
2. **Pre-Session Technical Verification:** Every speaker is given 15 minutes of uninterrupted stage time during the morning sound check to test display aspect ratios, IDE font sizing, and lapel microphone audio levels.
3. **Ceremonial Felicitation:** On stage, dignitaries present each speaker with a handcrafted local memento, traditional shawl, and framed certificate of appreciation.
4. **Follow-Up Thank-You:** Send high-resolution stage photos and audience feedback scores within 48 hours of conference completion.

---

## 3. The Attendee Experience Playbook

How to turn attendees into lifelong community advocates:
1. **Zero Check-In Friction:** Attendees present a digital QR code on their smartphone; registration volunteers scan it with mobile devices and hand over pre-printed badges in under 10 seconds.
2. **Continuous Hydration & Clear Signage:** Water dispensers placed outside every hall entrance; prominent directional standees at every corridor intersection.
3. **Accessible Resources:** All slide decks and demo source code repositories are published online within 24 hours of session completion.
4. **Instant Digital Certificates:** Automated certificate generation sends verified participation credentials directly to attendees' email addresses before they arrive home.

---

## 4. The Volunteer Motivation Playbook

How to run a team of 40+ student volunteers smoothly:
1. **Shift Rotations:** Never force student volunteers to work the entire 10 hours continuously. Rotate teams between morning and afternoon shifts so everyone attends sessions.
2. **Official Uniforms:** Provide matching club volunteer t-shirts and walkie-talkies to instill pride and clear visual identification for attendees.
3. **Daily Debrief & Appreciation:** Conclude the event with an internal team dinner, group photograph, and personalized Certificates of Organizing Excellence awarded by the Department Head.
