# Legal, Compliance & Risk Management

Operating a large-scale regional conference requires rigorous compliance with institutional statutes, corporate trademark rules, data privacy laws, and attendee safety protocols. This document outlines the legal and risk management framework for **.NET Conf Amravati**.

---

## 1. Institutional Governance & Approvals

All activities associated with .NET Conf Amravati occur under the formal authority of **P. R. Pote (Patil) College of Engineering and Management, Amravati**:

- **College Approval Sanction:** Every edition must receive written sanction from the Principal and Academic Council prior to public ticketing or corporate outreach.
- **Venue Sanction:** Facilities, power grids, and laboratories are secured through formal requisitions submitted to the Campus Estate Office.
- **Financial Authorization:** All cash sponsorships and vendor disbursements must flow through audited institutional student activity accounts under the supervision of the Faculty Advisor.

## 2. Microsoft Brand & Trademark Compliance

::: danger Mandatory Trademark & Disclaimer Rules
.NET Conf Amravati is an autonomous, community-driven conference organized independently by Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM. The organizing team must adhere strictly to Microsoft Community Trademark Guidelines:
:::

### Mandatory Legal Guidelines
1. **Disclaimer Requirement:** The following legal disclaimer must be displayed prominently on the official website, posters, and printed slide decks:
   > *".NET Conf Amravati is an independent community conference organized by Microsoft Student Club PRPCEM. It is not sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated directly with Microsoft Corporation, except through community event partnerships. Microsoft, .NET, Azure, and Windows are registered trademarks of Microsoft Corporation."*
2. **Logo Integrity:** The official Microsoft 4-color corporate logo may not be modified, cropped, recolored, or combined with other graphics.
3. **No False Endorsement:** Organizers may never represent the conference as an "Official Microsoft Event" or issue certificates stating "Certified by Microsoft" unless formal, written Microsoft certification partnership agreements are executed.

## 3. Attendee Privacy & Data Protection Policy

MSC PRPCEM strictly safeguards participant data in compliance with digital personal data protection principles:

- **Minimal Data Collection:** Registration forms collect only information essential to event logistics: Full Name, College/Organization, Academic Year, Email Address, and City.
- **Zero Commercial Sale:** Attendee lists, phone numbers, and resumes are **NEVER sold, rented, or distributed** to third parties or commercial marketing firms.
- **Sponsor Recruitment Safeguards:** Resumes may only be collected directly and voluntarily by sponsors at their physical exhibition booths in the presence of the candidate.
- **Photography & Recording Consent Notice:** A prominent notice is placed at the entrance:
  > *"By entering .NET Conf Amravati, you acknowledge that photography, audio, and video recording may occur. These assets are used solely for educational documentation, community archives, and non-commercial promotion by MSC PRPCEM."*

## 4. Conference Code of Conduct

.NET Conf Amravati is dedicated to providing a safe, inclusive, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, or technical proficiency.

### Expected Standards
- Treat all speakers, attendees, student volunteers, and staff with professional courtesy and respect.
- Provide constructive, respectful feedback during technical Q&A sessions.
- Refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior, language, or imagery.

### Violations & Incident Protocol
Any participant who engages in harassing or disruptive behavior will be warned immediately by the Safety Committee. Persistent misconduct will result in immediate expulsion from the premises without refund, and report to college disciplinary authorities.

## 6. Incident Reporting Protocol

1. **Incident Occurrence:** Any volunteer noticing a security, medical, or technical incident immediately alerts their Track Lead via walkie-talkie Channel 1.
2. **First Responder Assessment:** The designated Safety Lead arrives at the location within 2 minutes to evaluate whether medical or security intervention is required.
3. **Executive Escalation:** Incidents involving physical injury or major misconduct are escalated directly to the General Chair and Faculty Advisor.
4. **Post-Event Incident Logging:** A formal incident report detailing time, individuals involved, resolution, and future prevention recommendations must be appended to the official [Edition Dossier](/dotnet-conf/editions/).
