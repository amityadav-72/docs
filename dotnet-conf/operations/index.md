## 3. General Principles of Execution

1. **Redundancy Across Critical Systems:** Every single point of potential failure (audio cables, presentation laptops, stage internet, keynote backup speakers) must have an active secondary fallback.
2. **Strict Time Discipline:** Sessions start and conclude according to the published agenda without exception. Overrunning sessions infringes upon subsequent speakers and damages conference credibility.
3. **Attendee Safety & Dignity First:** Overcrowding beyond designated auditorium fire limits is strictly prohibited. Crowd flow must be actively regulated at all entrances.
