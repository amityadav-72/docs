## 3. Volunteer Recruitment & Deployment

Each conference edition deploys a dedicated cohort of 35 to 45 student volunteers selected through a competitive internal screening process:

### Volunteer Teams & Shift Rotations
1. **Registration & Check-In Desk (6 Volunteers):** Manage barcode/QR scanners, distribute delegate badges and kits, and resolve ticketing discrepancies.
2. **Auditorium Protocol & Stage Management (4 Volunteers):** Manage stage water service, podium mic adjustments, timer displays, and speaker memento handovers.
3. **Audience Q&A Runners (4 Volunteers):** Stationed in auditorium aisles with wireless handheld microphones for rapid audience Q&A delivery.
4. **Sponsor & Exhibition Hall Liaisons (6 Volunteers):** Assist corporate partners with booth setup, power connections, student queue control, and promotional flyer restocking.
5. **Catering & Hospitality Assistants (8 Volunteers):** Manage dining pavilion access, verify meal coupons, coordinate water distribution, and oversee clean-up.
6. **Guest Concierge & Escorts (4 Senior Volunteers):** Assigned 1-on-1 to invited outstation speakers for airport/railway reception and campus orientation.
7. **Media & Photography Crew (4 Volunteers):** High-resolution still photography, video recording, live social media story updates, and audio recording.

::: tip Volunteer Shift Balance
Volunteers work in two alternating 3-hour shifts (Morning Shift: 08:00 AM – 01:30 PM; Afternoon Shift: 01:30 PM – 06:30 PM). This guarantees that every student volunteer can attend at least half the technical sessions.
:::
