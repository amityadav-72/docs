## 2. Vendor Procurement SOPs
1. **Three-Quotation Rule:** Any single procurement exceeding ₹10,000 must obtain at least three competitive formal quotations before financial authorization.
2. **Quality Over Lowest Bid:** Selection criteria prioritize past reliability, hygiene standards, and responsiveness rather than marginal cost differences.
3. **Formal Work Orders:** No vendor may initiate work without a written, countersigned College Purchase Order (PO).
