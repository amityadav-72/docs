# Chapter 2: Organizational Structure

## 2.1 Club Organizational Hierarchy

MSC PRPCEM follows a structured hierarchy to ensure effective coordination, accountability, and smooth club operations.

**Organizational Hierarchy:**

1. **Dean Student Welfare**
2. **Faculty Coordinator**
3. **President**
4. **Vice President**
5. **Treasurer**
6. **Department Leads**
7. **Team Members**

---

## 2.2 Leadership Structure

The leadership team is responsible for managing club activities, coordinating departments, making decisions, and ensuring that the club achieves its objectives.

### President
- Oversees overall club operations.
- Defines annual goals and priorities.
- Coordinates with faculty and institutional authorities.
- Reviews major initiatives and decisions.

### Vice President
- Supports the President in club operations.
- Coordinates departments and monitors progress.
- Ensures smooth execution of planned activities.
- Handles responsibilities delegated by the President.

### Treasurer
- Manages club budgets and financial records.
- Reviews expenses and reimbursement requests.
- Tracks event-wise expenditure.
- Prepares financial reports.

---

## 2.3 Faculty Coordinator

The Faculty Coordinator provides institutional guidance and oversight.

**Responsibilities:**
- Guides club activities and initiatives.
- Coordinates institutional approvals.
- Ensures compliance with college policies.
- Supports the leadership team when required.

---

## 2.4 Department Structure

MSC PRPCEM's operational responsibilities are organized into the following departments:

| Department | Primary Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Technical** | Technical sessions, projects, and development |
| **Design & Creativity** | Visual assets and branding |
| **Content & Documentation** | Content writing, reports, and documentation |
| **Events & Logistics** | Event planning and execution |
| **Social Media** | Promotions and community engagement |
| **Outreach & Partnerships** | Speakers and external collaborations |
| **Sponsorship** | Sponsorship proposals and coordination |

---

## 2.5 Department Leads

Department Leads are responsible for managing their respective teams:

- Assign tasks and responsibilities.
- Monitor progress and deadlines.
- Maintain quality standards.
- Coordinate with club leadership.
- Report updates and challenges.
- Ensure proper documentation and handover.

---

## 2.6 Team Members

Team Members contribute to club activities based on their assigned departments:

- Complete assigned tasks on time.
- Attend required meetings and activities.
- Communicate progress and challenges.
- Follow club guidelines and procedures.
- Collaborate with other members.
- Maintain documentation of completed work.

---

## 2.7 Reporting and Communication

The general reporting structure is:

> **Team Member → Department Lead → Vice President / President → Faculty Coordinator → Institutional Authorities**

- Members should normally report to their Department Leads.
- Department Leads provide updates to club leadership.
- Major decisions requiring institutional authorization must follow the approved process.
- Urgent or sensitive concerns may be escalated directly to an appropriate authorized person.

---

## 2.8 Decision-Making and Approvals

| Decision | Responsible Authority |
|---|---|
| **Routine department tasks** | Department Lead |
| **Cross-department coordination** | Vice President / President |
| **Event proposals** | Club Leadership and required institutional authorities |
| **Budget and expenses** | Treasurer and designated approvers |
| **External partnerships** | President and required institutional authorities |
| **Major club decisions** | President with required institutional oversight |

Approval authority must follow the club's current institutional policies.

---

## 2.9 Meetings and Coordination

- **Department Meetings:** Task planning and progress updates.
- **Leadership Meetings:** Reviews, decisions, and upcoming initiatives.
- **Event Meetings:** Planning, execution, and coordination.
- **Review Meetings:** Performance, feedback, and improvements.

Important decisions and assigned action items should be documented.

---

## 2.10 Leadership Transition

At the end of a term, outgoing leaders should:

- Transfer ongoing responsibilities.
- Share relevant documents and records.
- Provide details of pending tasks and upcoming events.
- Transfer authorized access to tools and platforms.
- Brief incoming leaders on important processes.

*A proper handover ensures continuity between successive teams.*
