# Event Operations Checklist

*Actionable checklist across Pre-Event (T-14), D-Day stage execution, and Post-Event wrap-up.*

## Pre-Event Checklist (T-14 to T-1 Days)
- [ ] Obtain Principal & HoD signed permission letter with inward stamp
- [ ] Submit Central Computing Center lab requisition for workstations
- [ ] Submit Audio-Visual requisition for podium mic and projector
- [ ] Confirm speaker flight/train itinerary, bio, and presentation slides
- [ ] Finalize stage backdrop banner (8x4 ft) and social media announcement flyers
- [ ] Launch registration form and conduct classroom pitches
- [ ] Conduct lab dry run 48 hours prior (software runtimes, Wi-Fi whitelist)
- [ ] Procure green floral saplings / mementos and stage water bottles

## D-Day Stage Execution Checklist
- [ ] Hall unlocked and AV soundcheck conducted 60 minutes prior
- [ ] Registration desk operational at venue entrance with attendance sheets
- [ ] President & VP welcome Chief Guest at campus gate
- [ ] Stage inauguration: Saraswati Vandana and dignitary felicitation
- [ ] DSLR event photography covering speakers and student lab screens
- [ ] VP delivers formal Vote of Thanks and organizes crowd for group photo
- [ ] Project feedback QR code onto main screen before students depart

## Post-Event Wrap-up (T+1 to T+3 Days)
- [ ] Cross-match physical attendance register against registration submissions
- [ ] Generate and email QR-verified digital certificates
- [ ] Compile 4-page Post-Event Report (PER) and submit hardcopy to Principal
- [ ] Submit original vendor GST receipts to college accounts section
- [ ] Publish LinkedIn and Instagram recap posts tagging PRPCEM and Microsoft
- [ ] Send formal letter of appreciation to the guest speaker
