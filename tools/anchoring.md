# Stage Anchoring Prompter

*Complete spoken scripts, timing cues, and stage protocol for event anchors.*

## 1. Opening Address & Stage Call (10:00 AM)
::: tip Stage & Audio Cue
Soft background tech instrumental fades down. Spotlights on podium.
:::

> "A very warm and delightful morning to our honorable dignitaries on the dais, respected Principal Sir, respected Head of Department, our enthusiastic Faculty Coordinator, our esteemed guest speaker of the day, and my dear fellow engineers!
>
> *'Technology is best when it brings people together.'*
>
> On behalf of the Microsoft Student Club (MSC) of P. R. Pote Patil College of Engineering & Management, Amravati, I take immense pride and honor in welcoming each and every one of you to our flagship technical workshop! Today is not just about lectures; it is about building, deploying, and stepping into the future of cloud computing and intelligence."

---

## 2. Lighting of the Lamp (Deep Prajwalan) (10:05 AM)
::: tip Stage & Audio Cue
Play Saraswati Vandana instrumental track. Volunteers escort dignitaries with brass lamp and matches.
:::

> *"Asato ma sadgamaya, Tamaso ma jyotirgamaya."*  
> Knowledge is the divine beacon that dispels the darkness of ignorance.
>
> May I now respectfully request our honorable Principal, respected HoD Sir, Faculty Coordinator, and our chief guest to step forward for the auspicious lighting of the lamp and seek the blessings of Goddess Saraswati.
>
> *[Pause while lamp is lit by dignitaries]*
>
> Thank you respected dignitaries. Please grace your respective seats on the dais.

---

## 3. Dignitary Felicitation with Floral Saplings (10:10 AM)
::: tip Stage & Audio Cue
Logistics volunteers bring indoor green saplings and mementos on silver trays from stage left.
:::

> In Indian tradition, we honor our guests with green saplings that symbolize perpetual growth, learning, and sustainability.
>
> • May I request our President, MSC PRPCEM, to welcome and felicitate our honorable Principal with a green sapling. *(Applause)*  
> • May I request our Vice President to welcome our respected HoD. *(Applause)*  
> • And now, the moment we have all eagerly awaited: may I request our respected Principal Sir to kindly felicitate our Chief Guest and Resource Person for today with a sapling and token of college esteem! *(Heavy Applause)*

---

## 4. Formal Vote of Thanks (Valedictory) (04:15 PM)
> *"Gratitude is the memory of the heart."*
>
> As we draw curtains on this transformative edition of today's workshop, I stand before you filled with profound gratitude.
>
> First and foremost, our heartfelt thanks to our mentor and Chief Guest for his invaluable time, hands-on guidance, and for demystifying complex cloud concepts with such clarity.
>
> We express our sincerest gratitude to our visionary Principal and HoD, whose unwavering administrative support and faith in student initiatives make events like MSC possible.
>
> A huge shoutout to our incredible core team—Technical, Design, PR, and Logistics leads—who worked tirelessly to ensure today went without a single glitch.
>
> And finally, to you—our dear participants. Your relentless curiosity, active debugging, and vibrant enthusiasm are the true heartbeat of MSC PRPCEM. Thank you one and all!
