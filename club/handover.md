# Leadership Transition & Handover Protocol

## Succession Calendar
- **March 15:** Succession announcement & nomination invitations.
- **April 01–10:** Core team interview panels conducted with outgoing leads and Faculty Advisor.
- **April 15:** Announcement of incoming Executive Board.
- **April 15–30:** 2-week shadow period where incoming leads observe all executive operations.
- **April 30:** Signing of official Tenure Handover Memorandum (`TMPL-22`) and credential transfer.

## Asset Transfer Checklist
- [ ] Domain DNS control for `mscprpcem.tech` and `docs.mscprpcem.tech`.
- [ ] Official Google Workspace / Email credentials.
- [ ] GitHub Organization Owner transfer (`github.com/mscprpcem`).
- [ ] Microsoft Azure for Students subscription & Sponsorship credits portal.
- [ ] Canva Pro for Teams login and master brand kit folders.
- [ ] Physical documentation binder (approval letters, PERs, invoices).
