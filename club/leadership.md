# Club Leadership Structure

## Executive Board Structure
The executive leadership consists of seven core offices:

| Role | Domain | Primary Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **President** | Executive Board | Principal liaison with Principal, HoDs, and industry partners |
| **Vice President** | Operations & Alignment | Oversees cross-team deliverables and master calendar |
| **Technical Lead** | Tech & Architecture | Maintains GitHub org, repos, and workshop lab setups |
| **Event & Logistics Lead** | Operations & Venue | Handles hall bookings, sound, projectors, and stage flow |
| **Design & Media Lead** | Creative & Branding | Produces banners, event flyers, badges, and photography |
| **PR & Outreach Lead** | Communications | Manages social media, class announcements, and PR |
| **Sponsorship & Finance Lead** | Treasurer | Manages budget estimates, receipts, and college account settlement |

## Institutional Advisory Board
- **Chief Patron:** Principal, PRPCEM Amravati
- **Academic Patron:** Head of Department, CSE / IT
- **Faculty Coordinator:** Senior Faculty Member, MSC PRPCEM
