# Compliance & Safety Standards

## Mandatory Compliance
1. **14-Day Paperwork Window:** No campus event may be publicized without formal written approval from the Principal obtained 14 days in advance.
2. **Lab Safety & Property:** No physical reconfiguration of campus desktop hardware or lab networking is permitted.
3. **On-Duty Attendance (OD):** OD leave is strictly reserved for active student organizers and requires verified Faculty Advisor endorsement.
