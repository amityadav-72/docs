# Roles & Responsibilities Charter

## Executive Responsibilities
- **President:** Strategic vision, Principal permissions, budget sign-off, external sponsor partnerships.
- **Vice President:** Milestone tracking, cross-domain resolution, volunteer coordination.
- **Technical Lead:** Repository governance, code reviews, workshop lab curriculum.
- **Event Operations Lead:** Hall requisitions, sound and mic setups, stage schedule.
- **Design Lead:** Brand standards, visual collaterals, official photography.
- **PR Lead:** Class outreach pitches, WhatsApp broadcasts, social media recap.
- **Finance Lead:** Itemized ledgers, receipt audit, accounts department settlement.
