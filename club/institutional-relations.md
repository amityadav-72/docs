# Institutional Relations & Protocol

## Administrative Hierarchy
1. **Principal:** Chief executive sanction authority for all events and expenditures on campus.
2. **Head of Department (CSE / IT):** Recommends department lab allocations and academic attendance regularizations.
3. **Faculty Advisor:** Appointed faculty member directly mentoring club execution.

## Inward Letter Register
All official letters submitted to the Principal's office must be registered in the college inward diary to establish verified record trail.
