# Mission & Objectives

## Mission
To cultivate an engineering culture of practical building, rigorous software standards, collaborative problem-solving, and continuous learning among engineering students at PRPCEM.

## Key Targets
- Conduct at least 3 flagship, multi-day technical workshops annually where at least 70% of instructional time is dedicated to live coding.
- Host the annual 24-hour inter-college hackathon (InnovateX) featuring real-world problem statements and industry mentorship.
- Ensure all software tools developed for internal campus utility (e.g., club portals, certificates, attendance tools) are maintained under strict CI/CD pipelines.
