## Institutional Identity
Microsoft Student Club (MSC PRPCEM) is the official student developer community operating at **P. R. Pote (Patil) College of Engineering and Management (PRPCEM)**, Amravati, Maharashtra.

Operating under the mentorship of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering and Information Technology, MSC PRPCEM serves as a collaborative bridge between undergraduate engineers and modern industry cloud ecosystems.

## Core Pillars
1. **Industry-Grade Technical Skills:** Training students in full-stack web development, Azure cloud services, AI/ML, and DevOps.
2. **Project-Driven Engineering:** Mentoring student software developers to build production-grade applications.
3. **Open-Source Leadership:** Maintaining public repositories under `github.com/mscprpcem` with rigorous peer reviews and branch protections.
4. **Professional Governance:** Operating an organized, accountable leadership body with transparent budgeting, verified documentation, and orderly annual transitions.

## Institutional Affiliation
- **Institution:** P. R. Pote (Patil) College of Engineering and Management, Amravati
- **Approved By:** AICTE New Delhi | Affiliated to SGBAU Amravati | NAAC 'A+' Accredited
