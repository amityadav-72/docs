# Meeting Documentation Procedures

## Meeting Protocol
- **Agenda Circulation:** Meeting chair must circulate an agenda at least 24 hours prior to regular scrums.
- **Minutes of Meeting (MoM):** Documentation team must record attendees, decisions, and action items using [TMPL-13](/templates/meeting-minutes).
- **Distribution:** MoM must be shared with all attendees within 24 hours of meeting completion.
