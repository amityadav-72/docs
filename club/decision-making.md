# Decision-Making Guidelines

## Decision Authority Tiers
- **Tier 1 (Operational):** Domain Leads decide domain tasks independently (e.g., choice of workshop library, flyer color layout).
- **Tier 2 (Event & Budget):** Requires simple majority vote of the Executive Board (President + VP + 5 Leads).
- **Tier 3 (Constitutional / Policy):** Requires unanimous Executive Board consent and written approval from Faculty Advisor.
