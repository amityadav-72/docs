# Architectural Decision Record (ADR) Template

`Template ID: TMPL-05`

```markdown
# ADR [Number]: [Title of Decision]

## Status
Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-XXX]

## Context
What technical challenge, scalability issue, or constraint prompted this decision?

## Decision
What is the chosen approach, library, or architectural pattern?

## Consequences
### Positive Outcomes:
- Advantage 1
- Advantage 2

### Negative / Trade-offs:
- Trade-off 1
- Mitigation strategy

## Stakeholders & Approvals
- Proposed by: [Name, Role]
- Approved by: [Technical Lead, Date]
```
