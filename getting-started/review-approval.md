# Documentation Review and Approval Process

## Approval Workflow

1. **Author Submits PR:** Markdown changes submitted targeting the `main` branch.
2. **Technical & Factual Check:** Respective domain lead confirms accuracy and ensures zero secrets or hardcoded passwords are included.
3. **Documentation Lead Verification:** Verifies formatting, header anchors, and working links.
4. **Merge & Live Deployment:** Once approved, merging deploys the update immediately to `docs.mscprpcem.tech`.
