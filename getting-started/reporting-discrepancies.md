# Reporting Incorrect or Outdated Documentation

If you encounter an outdated command, broken link, or inaccurate procedure:
1. Open a GitHub Issue at `github.com/mscprpcem/docs/issues`.
2. Title it `[Discrepancy]: Document Title (ID)`.
3. Provide the exact section with the error and the verified current facts.
4. Or directly click **"Edit this page on GitHub"** at the bottom of the page to submit a proposed fix.
