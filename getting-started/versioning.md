# Document Versioning and Revision History

## Semantic Versioning for Docs
- **MAJOR (`v2.0.0`):** Full rewrite or replacement of a procedure.
- **MINOR (`v1.1.0`):** Addition of new steps, checklists, or sections.
- **PATCH (`v1.0.1`):** Typo fixes, dead link corrections, or wording clarity.

Changelogs are maintained in each document's revision history table and summarized globally in the [Master Changelog](/archive/changelog).
