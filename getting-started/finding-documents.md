# How to Find a Document

## Search Shortcuts
- Press `Ctrl + K` (Windows) or `Cmd + K` (Mac) anywhere on the portal to open the instant search popup.
- Type any procedure name (e.g., `permission`, `azure`, `workshop`) or Document ID (e.g., `SOP-01`, `POL-01`).

## Browsing by Category
Use the left sidebar menu to navigate through the 11 organized categories. All SOPs are indexed under **7. Standard Operating Procedures**, and binding rules under **8. Policies & Guidelines**.
