## About the Club

**Microsoft Student Club (MSC PRPCEM)** is an institutionally recognized student technical community operating at P. R. Pote (Patil) College of Engineering and Management, Amravati.

The club bridges the gap between academic curriculum and high-impact industry engineering practices by providing hands-on exposure to Microsoft technologies, Azure cloud infrastructure, AI models, modern web frameworks, and collaborative open-source workflows.

### Core Mission & Pillars

1. **Practical Technical Mastery:** Moving beyond theory to build production-grade web applications, cloud backends, and AI solutions.
2. **High-Impact Events & Bootcamps:** Organizing 70% hands-on coding workshops, campus hackathons, and technical symposiums led by Microsoft MVPs and industry architects.
3. **Institutional Excellence & Standard Procedures:** Ensuring 100% adherence to college permissions, lab guidelines, and professional accountability.
4. **Transparent Leadership & Continuity:** Documenting all workflows so every incoming leadership batch has immediate operational clarity.
