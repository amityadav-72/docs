# Speaker Outreach & Coordination

## Outreach Timeline
- **T-21 Days:** Send formal invitation letter specifying topic, expected student audience profile, and date options.
- **T-7 Days:** Collect high-resolution headshot, speaker bio, and presentation slides.
- **T-2 Days:** Confirm transportation, airport/railway pickup, and stage arrival time.
- **D-Day:** President and VP receive guest at campus gate; escort to Principal's office for tea before session.
- **T+2 Days:** Dispatch formal letter of appreciation signed by Principal and President.
