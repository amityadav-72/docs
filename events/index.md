## The 6-Phase Lifecycle

```
Phase 1: Ideation (T-30 to T-21 Days)
   ↓
Phase 2: Administrative Permissions (T-20 to T-14 Days)
   ↓
Phase 3: Marketing & Registrations (T-14 to T-3 Days)
   ↓
Phase 4: Technical & Stage Rehearsal (T-48 to T-2 Hours)
   ↓
Phase 5: D-Day Execution (Event Day)
   ↓
Phase 6: Post-Event Wrap-up (T+1 to T+3 Days)
```
