# Venue & Equipment Checklists

## Pre-Event Verification
- [ ] Hall reserved in college master diary; keys access confirmed with peon staff.
- [ ] Podium microphone tested for clear vocal reproduction without feedback whistle.
- [ ] 2 Wireless handheld microphones loaded with fresh batteries.
- [ ] Projector resolution configured to 16:9; HDMI cords and USB-C adapters verified.
- [ ] Air conditioning switched on 30 minutes before commencement.
- [ ] Clean glasses of drinking water and paper napkins placed on stage table.
