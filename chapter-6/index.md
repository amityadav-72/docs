# Chapter 6: Event Management

## 6.1 Purpose

This chapter defines the standard process for planning, approving, executing, and documenting events organized by MSC PRPCEM.

---

## 6.2 Event Proposal

Every proposed event should include:

- Event name and type.
- Objectives and expected outcomes.
- Target audience.
- Proposed date, time, and venue.
- Estimated budget and resource requirements.
- Organizing team and responsibilities.

---

## 6.3 Event Approval

- Submit the proposal to club leadership.
- Obtain required faculty and institutional approvals.
- Confirm budget authorization before making financial commitments.
- Finalize the event only after the necessary approvals.

---

## 6.4 Event Planning

After approval:

- Assign an event lead and organizing team.
- Prepare a timeline and task list.
- Confirm speakers, mentors, and collaborators.
- Arrange venue, equipment, and other resources.
- Prepare registration and promotional materials.
- Identify potential risks and backup arrangements.

---

## 6.5 Promotion and Registration

- Publish approved event announcements.
- Provide accurate event details and registration instructions.
- Track registrations and communicate updates.
- Protect participant information.
- Send reminders and relevant joining instructions.

---

## 6.6 Event-Day Execution

The organizing team should:

- Arrive early and check the venue and equipment.
- Confirm speaker and volunteer availability.
- Manage registration and participant assistance.
- Follow the event schedule.
- Handle technical or logistical issues.
- Record relevant attendance and event outcomes.

---

## 6.7 Budget and Expenses

- Follow the approved event budget.
- Obtain required authorization before spending.
- Collect receipts and supporting documents.
- Submit expenses to the Treasurer or designated approver.
- Record final expenditure and any remaining funds according to club procedures.

---

## 6.8 Post-Event Activities

After the event:

- Share approved follow-up communications.
- Collect participant feedback.
- Process certificates, where applicable.
- Prepare the event report.
- Reconcile expenses.
- Archive promotional materials and event records.
- Record lessons learned and pending actions.

---

## 6.9 Event Report

Each event report should contain:

- Event name, date, and venue.
- Objectives and organizing team.
- Speakers and collaborators.
- Registration and attendance figures, where available.
- Activities conducted and outcomes achieved.
- Budgeted versus actual expenditure.
- Feedback and key learnings.
- Links to relevant photographs, presentations, and resources.

---

## 6.10 Cancellation or Postponement

If an event must be postponed or cancelled:

- Inform club leadership and obtain required approvals.
- Notify speakers, participants, and collaborators promptly.
- Update official event listings and communication channels.
- Coordinate refunds or financial adjustments where applicable.
- Document the reason and revised plans.
