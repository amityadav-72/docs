# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

*Standard Operating Procedures provide definitive, step-by-step instructions for recurring administrative and technical workflows at MSC PRPCEM.*

::: tip Canonical 14-Point Standard
Every official SOP in this section strictly adheres to the 14-field schema: Title & Identifier, Purpose, Scope, Responsible Role, Prerequisites, Required Approvals, Step-by-Step Procedure, Expected Outcome, Verification Checklist, Exception Handling, Escalation, Related Documents, Owner & Review Interval, and Revision History.
:::
