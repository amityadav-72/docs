# SOP-GOV-01: Executing Annual Leadership Handover

## 1. SOP Title and Identifier
**SOP-GOV-01: Executing Annual Leadership Handover**

## 2. Purpose
To govern the orderly, transparent transition of executive authority, digital domain DNS credentials, financial records, and physical archives between tenures.

## 3. Scope
Annual transition between outgoing and incoming Executive Board members of MSC PRPCEM.

## 4. Responsible Role
**President, MSC PRPCEM**

## 5. Prerequisites
- Completed year-end recruitment interviews and selection of incoming board.
- Reconciled financial accounts and cleared vendor vouchers.

## 6. Required Approvals
1. Outgoing President.
2. Incoming President.
3. Faculty Coordinator, MSC PRPCEM.

## 7. Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Dossier Audit (April 15):** Compile physical binder of all approved Principal letters, financial vouchers, and inventory.
2. **Digital Transition (April 20):** Transfer Owner access for `github.com/mscprpcem`, Cloudflare DNS (`mscprpcem.tech`), Google Workspace, and LinkedIn/Instagram.
3. **Walkthrough Meetings (April 22–28):** Conduct domain-specific operational handover sessions.
4. **Attestation (April 30):** Execute physical signing of the Tenure Handover Memorandum (`TMPL-22`) in the presence of the Faculty Advisor.

## 8. Expected Outcome
Complete, peaceful transfer of administrative credentials, financial balance sheets, and active projects.

## 9. Verification Checklist
- [ ] Domain DNS transferred.
- [ ] GitHub Org Owner assigned.
- [ ] Financial balance verified with Faculty Advisor.
- [ ] Memorandum signed and archived.

## 10. Exception Handling
If dispute arises during account handover, the Faculty Advisor serves as final arbitrator.

## 11. Escalation Procedure
Escalate unresolved succession disputes to the Head of Department.
